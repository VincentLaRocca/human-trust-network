# Personal chains

One contract, one history, held by the parties who receive the consignment.

Specified, not encoded:

- HardwareTitleDeed_v1 — one title right
- CopyrightMaster_v1 — master right plus capped license rights
- SovereignAgent_v1 — control right plus capped instance rights

A transition cannot update a seal in place. Issuing a license or an instance closes the parent seal and reopens it.
